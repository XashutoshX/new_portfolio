01The Pivot

I'm an analytical, outcome-oriented Product Manager with 5.5+ years of experience. I started in data science - three years deep in chatbots, ML pipelines, and predictive models, until a set of KPI dashboards I built almost as an afterthought became the standard decision-making tool for leadership across three business verticals at Accenture. That's when I realized: the model was never the point. The decision was. I made the shift to product formal with a certification from ISB.

Since then, I've led initiatives in conversational AI, data-driven growth, and predictive analytics - improving product performance, reducing costs, and boosting engagement. I'm hands-on with roadmapping, user research, and go-to-market strategy, and I work closely with cross-functional teams to keep product vision tied to business goals.
